✍️ Poem — English & Nepali

As tomorrow's winds whisper low and sweet,

Kathmandu's misty veil will gently meet,

The chill of morning, with a warmth that clings,

Under a sky of grey, where clouds convene in rings.

In Biratnagar, the air is thick and warm,

With scents of rain, and a cloudy form,

Aalborg's breeze is crisp, with a lighter touch,

And though the skies are grey, its chill is gentle as a clutch.

The gentle breeze and warmer touch of Kathmandu's air,

Make it the nicest to be in, without a single care.
