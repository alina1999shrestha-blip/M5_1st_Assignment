In Kathmandu, the sky is veiled in grey,

A soothing mist that brings a calm display,

The gentle breeze whispers secrets, cool and low,

As petrichor awakens, scenting the air to and fro.

In Biratnagar, warmth abounds, with winds that sway,

A lively dance of heat, on this summer's day,

While Aalborg's chill begins to seep, with gusts that bite,

A stark contrast to the others, in this weather's plight.

The warmth of Biratnagar calls, a haven from the grey,

A place to bask, and let the senses sway.

Kathmandu's calm, and Aalborg's chill, can't compare,

To Biratnagar's vibrant beat, that's beyond compare.
