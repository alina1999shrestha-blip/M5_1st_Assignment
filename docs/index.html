✍️ Poem — English & Nepali

In Kathmandu, the sky is veiled in misty grey,

A soothing chill in the morning air to stay.

The breeze is gentle, whispers through the trees,

As rain drums on the rooftops with gentle ease.

In Biratnagar, warmth awakens, a vibrant hue,

The wind stirs, carrying scents of blooming dew.

Aalborg's sky is partly shrouded, with a crisp, cool light,

Inviting all to bask in its refreshing sight.

Among these three, Aalborg's gentle chill I'd chase,

For a serene and peaceful day, it's the perfect place.
