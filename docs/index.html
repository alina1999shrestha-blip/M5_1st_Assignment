✍️ Poem — English & Nepali

In Kathmandu's misty veil, the air is thick with scent,

A gentle breeze stirs, whispers secrets, as the rain descends.

Biratnagar's windswept streets, a warmer, drier tale unfold,

With cloudy skies that weep, yet sunshine peeks, young and untold.

Aalborg's vast sky stretches, a deep blue, almost grey,

With warmth that beckons, and a chill that whispers, "come what may".

The Danish city's sun-kissed streets, a haven from the rain,

Might be the nicest refuge, where warmth and light entwine in vain.
