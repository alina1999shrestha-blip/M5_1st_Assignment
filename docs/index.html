✍️ Poem — English & Nepali

In Kathmandu's misty veil, the air is warm and still,

A gentle breeze stirs, with scents of wet earth's thrill.

Biratnagar swelters, a hot and humid space,

Where clouds congregate, and rain drums on the place.

Aalborg's sky is vast, with winds that whip and sway,

A chill awakens, as the sun's warm light gives way.

The Danish city's gusts invigorate, a refreshing treat,

While Nepal's twin cities simmer, in a sultry heat.

Tomorrow, I'd choose Aalborg's airy, open sky,

To breathe, to walk, to feel the wind's crisp sigh.
