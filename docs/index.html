In Kathmandu, the sky is veiled in grey,

A soothing mist that gently sways,

The breeze is soft, the temperature mild,

A gentle haven from the world's wild child.

In Biratnagar, the sun beats down with might,

Warmth on skin, a scent of blooming light,

Aalborg's chill is crisp, with winds that bite,

A stark contrast to the others' gentle night.

The warmth of Biratnagar's sunny rays,

Makes it the place to be on tomorrow's days.
