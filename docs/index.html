As morning dawns, Kathmandu's gentle breeze whispers low,

A soothing calm, with warmth that ebbs and flows.

In Biratnagar, heat envelops the day,

With winds that stir, and scorching light that sways.

Aalborg's chill, a contrasting tale unfolds,

With gusts that cut, and skies that weep with cold.

The scent of rain on Kathmandu's streets is sweet,

A haven from the heat, where heart and soul can meet.
